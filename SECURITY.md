# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem, such as an exploit, a way into someone else's account or a way to crash or take over the servers, please **do not open a public issue**. Anyone can read issues, so posting it there puts players at risk before we can fix it.

Email **[support@toontown.io](mailto:support@toontown.io)** instead, and include:

- What the problem is and what someone could do with it
- The steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- The game version, and your platform if the problem is on the client side

We'll reply to confirm we received your report and keep you updated while we work on a fix. Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix it before you tell anyone else.

## Supported versions

We only fix the current release on the live channel and the `master` branch. The historical branches in this repository are archived and don't get security fixes.
